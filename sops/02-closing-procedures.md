# SOP 02 — Closing Procedures

**Goal:** All shipments dispatched or staged, money reconciled and secured,
systems shut down, and the store locked and armed. Begin closing tasks about
**`[CONFIRM: 15–20]` minutes** before close, but **do not lock out a customer
who is already inside or being served.**

## 1. Final customers & shipments

1. Serve any remaining customers. Stop accepting new walk‑ins at closing time.
2. Confirm all of today's outbound packages are scanned, manifested, and either
   picked up or staged for tomorrow's first pickup.
3. Confirm all prepaid drop‑offs and Amazon/QR returns were scanned/accepted so
   customers get drop‑off confirmation.

## 2. Reconcile the register

1. Close out the POS for the day; print the end‑of‑day / Z report.
2. Count the drawer. Separate the **`[CONFIRM: $150]` starting bank** from the
   day's sales.
3. Compare counted cash to the POS report. Record both on the
   [Cash Handling log](10-cash-handling-pos.md).
4. If over/short by more than **`[CONFIRM: $2]`**, recount and note the variance
   and any explanation. Report large variances to the manager same day.
5. Reset the starting bank for tomorrow. Place deposit cash in the
   safe/deposit bag per [SOP 10](10-cash-handling-pos.md).

## 3. Carrier end‑of‑day

1. Run / confirm the USPS CPU end‑of‑day close and dispatch so today's packages
   show as accepted.
2. **Take the photo of the category-code report** and submit the digital
   end-of-day close-out form (see [tools/closeout-form](../tools/closeout-form/README.md)).
3. File or stage manifests and scan reports per record‑keeping rules.

## 4. Shut down & secure

1. Log out of all carrier systems and the POS.
2. Power down the copier, label printers, scales, and non‑essential equipment
   `[CONFIRM: which stay on overnight]`.
3. Secure all customer documents, IDs, notary journal, and originals in their
   proper locked location.
4. Empty trash and recycling; quick wipe of counters.
5. Bring in sidewalk signage; turn off open sign.

## 5. Lock up

1. Verify back door and any side doors are locked.
2. Turn off lights.
3. Set the alarm. Code: `[CONFIRM]`.
4. Exit and confirm the front door is locked and pulled secure.
5. Do not linger in the parking lot after dark; if you feel unsafe, leave and
   call the manager.

> **Closing checklist** (initial each night): shipments dispatched/staged · POS
> closed · drawer counted & reconciled · carriers end‑of‑day · documents
> secured · systems off · doors locked · alarm armed.
