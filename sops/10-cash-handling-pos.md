# SOP 10 — Cash Handling & Point of Sale

Money discipline protects the business and protects **you** from being blamed
for a shortage. Follow this exactly, every shift.

## 1. The cash drawer

- Starting bank: **`[CONFIRM: $150]`**, counted at open and reset at close
  ([SOP 01](01-opening-procedures.md) / [SOP 02](02-closing-procedures.md)).
- **One cashier per drawer per shift** where possible. If you didn't count it,
  recount it before you take responsibility.
- Keep the drawer **closed** between transactions. Never leave it open.
- Don't keep large bills in the drawer — drop **$50s and $100s** (and excess
  cash over `[CONFIRM: $200]`) into the safe/drop slot during the day.

## 2. Every transaction

1. **Quote the price first.** (See every service SOP.)
2. Enter the sale into the POS — ring up *everything*, including packing
   materials and service fees. No off‑system "cash on the side." Ever.
3. State the total clearly.
4. For cash: count the customer's money **out loud**, leave their bill on the
   drawer ledge until you've counted change back, then drop it in. This prevents
   "I gave you a $50" disputes.
5. Count change back to the customer.
6. Hand over the **receipt** with every sale.

## 3. Cards & digital payment

- Run cards through the POS/terminal. For chip/tap, let the customer insert/tap.
- We **do not** store or write down full card numbers.
- For declines, ask for another payment method politely; don't speculate why.
- Refunds to a card go back to the **same card** — never refund cash for a card
  sale.

## 4. Counterfeit & fraud

1. Check large bills (`$50`/`$100`) with the counterfeit pen / UV light.
2. If a bill looks fake: do **not** return it to the customer, do **not**
   accuse them. Say the policy requires manager verification, set it aside, and
   call the manager. If clearly counterfeit and the customer leaves, note a
   description and call the non‑emergency police line.
3. Watch for common scams: rushed customers asking for big change, distraction
   tactics, "I gave you a bigger bill" claims (this is why we count out loud and
   leave the bill on the ledge).

## 5. Refunds, voids, discounts

- Refunds and voids over **`[CONFIRM: $25]`** require **manager approval** and a
  reason recorded in the POS.
- USPS/CPU refunds follow USPS rules, not store cash‑back
  ([SOP 05](05-usps-cpu.md)).
- Employee discounts / freebies require manager approval and are rung up so
  they're tracked.

## 6. End of day reconciliation

1. Count the drawer; separate the starting bank from the day's takings.
2. Compare to the POS Z report; record on the **Cash Handling log**.
3. Over/short greater than **`[CONFIRM: $2]`** → recount, document, and notify
   the manager.
4. Prepare the deposit `[CONFIRM: deposit cadence & who takes it to the bank]`;
   place in the safe/deposit bag.
5. Reset tomorrow's bank and lock the till in the safe.
