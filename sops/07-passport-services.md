# SOP 07 — Passport Photos & Application Support

We provide **passport photos** and **help preparing passport paperwork**. We are
**not** a passport acceptance facility unless explicitly stated `[CONFIRM]`, so
be precise about what we do and don't do.

## 1. What we do / don't do

| We DO | We DON'T |
|---|---|
| Take compliant U.S. passport photos | Approve or issue passports |
| Help fill out forms (DS‑11, DS‑82) | Give immigration/legal advice |
| Print/copy supporting documents | Guarantee acceptance by the State Dept. |
| Explain where/how to submit | Mail the application unless they choose our shipping |

Always tell customers: **final acceptance and photo approval rest with the U.S.
Department of State / acceptance agent — not us.**

## 2. Passport photo standards (must‑pass)

Take photos to the official U.S. requirements:
- **2 x 2 inches**, color, taken within the last 6 months.
- Head height **1 to 1‑3/8 inches** from chin to top of head, centered.
- **Plain white or off‑white background**, no shadows.
- Neutral expression (or natural smile), **both eyes open**, facing camera.
- No glasses. No hats/head coverings (except daily religious wear with a signed
  statement). No uniforms.
- Even lighting, in focus, no red‑eye, no filters/digital alteration.

Workflow:
1. Seat the customer against the white backdrop with even lighting.
2. Take the photo; check head size, background, shadows, eyes, expression.
3. Reshoot until it passes — **better a reshoot than a rejected application.**
4. Print the **two** photos on photo paper, cut to 2x2.
5. Charge the posted passport‑photo price `[CONFIRM]`.

## 3. Helping with the application

1. Identify which form they need:
   - **DS‑11** — first‑time, minors, lost/stolen, or expired long ago / when
     you were under 16. Submitted **in person**.
   - **DS‑82** — eligible renewals by mail.
2. We can help them fill it out accurately, but **the applicant provides all the
   information and is responsible for its accuracy.** Don't guess on their
   behalf.
3. **Do not sign DS‑11 forms ahead of time** — DS‑11 must be signed in front of
   an acceptance agent, not at our counter.
4. Print/copy supporting docs (IDs, certificates) as a paid service
   ([SOP 08](08-print-copy-fax-scan.md)). Handle originals carefully and return
   them immediately.
5. Point them to the nearest **passport acceptance facility** (post office,
   clerk of court) for DS‑11 submission, and explain expedited vs. routine
   timelines exist but **we don't control State Dept. processing times.**

## 4. Privacy

Passport documents contain sensitive personal data. Never leave them on the
counter, never photograph/keep copies for ourselves, and shred any misprints
([SOP 09](09-shredding.md)).

> Get the photo right, help fill the form honestly, hand back every original,
> and be clear we don't issue the passport.
