# SOP 05 — USPS Contract Postal Unit (CPU)

We operate a USPS **Contract Postal Unit**. That means we sell postal products
and services on behalf of USPS and are held to **USPS standards and
accountability**. CPU funds and stock are tracked separately and audited — treat
this station with extra care.

> **Note:** We do **not** rent P.O. boxes. If a customer asks for a P.O. box,
> refer them to the local Post Office, or offer our shipping/mail services
> instead.

## 1. Products & services we provide

- Postage and stamps `[CONFIRM: which stamp denominations stocked]`
- USPS mail classes: First‑Class, Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express, Ground
  Advantage, Media Mail, etc.
- Flat‑rate Priority packaging (free USPS supplies — do **not** charge for the
  USPS flat‑rate boxes/envelopes themselves)
- Extra services: Certified Mail, Registered Mail, Return Receipt, Signature
  Confirmation, insurance
- Money orders `[CONFIRM: offered? max amount]`
- International mail and customs forms

## 2. Daily CPU accountability

1. At open, confirm the CPU stamp stock and cash accountability match the
   recorded balance. Record on the CPU log.
2. Keep **USPS funds/stamp stock accounting separate** from general store sales
   where required by our CPU agreement `[CONFIRM: separate drawer vs. tracked
   in POS]`.
3. At close, run the CPU end‑of‑day, reconcile stamp stock sold vs. cash
   received, and transmit/dispatch.
4. **Any shortage or overage in CPU funds is reported to the manager the same
   day** and documented. USPS audits these.

## 3. Selling postage / mailing a letter or package

1. Determine class and any extra services the customer needs.
2. Weigh and rate the item on the USPS terminal/scale.
3. Quote, collect payment, apply postage/label.
4. Provide tracking/receipt for any traceable service.
5. Place in the USPS outbound staging for dispatch.

## 4. Certified / Registered / accountable mail

- **Certified Mail:** complete the green card / electronic certified, give the
  customer the receipt with the article number. If Return Receipt is added,
  ensure it's attached correctly.
- **Registered Mail:** highest security — handle chain‑of‑custody carefully,
  declared value recorded, stored secured until dispatch.
- Never lose an accountable item's barcode/receipt; the customer's proof of
  mailing depends on it.

## 5. International mail & customs

1. Complete the correct customs form (electronic where required).
2. Customer must accurately declare **contents and value**; we do not falsify
   declarations (e.g., marking a sold item as "gift").
3. Check the country's prohibited/restricted list and any size/weight limits.
4. Provide tracking where the service includes it.

## 6. Money orders `[CONFIRM: if offered]`

1. Verify funds/payment first.
2. Fill amount accurately; once printed, errors are costly — double‑check.
3. Give the customer their copy/receipt.
4. Log per CPU money‑order procedures.

## 7. Refunds & voids

- Postage refunds follow **USPS rules**, not store discretion — most require a
  request through USPS, not a counter cash‑back.
- Voiding an unused label: follow the terminal's void process and document it.
- When in doubt, escalate to the manager rather than refunding cash.

> CPU work is USPS's money and reputation, audited and accountable. Reconcile
> daily, document every shortage, and never falsify a customs form.
