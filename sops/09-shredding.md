# SOP 09 — Document Shredding

We offer secure document shredding `[CONFIRM: on‑site shredder vs. bonded
third‑party bin]`. The customer is paying for **security** — handle their
documents as confidential from the counter to destruction.

## 1. Intake & quote

1. Confirm whether they want **shred‑by‑weight/volume** or a flat per‑item
   price `[CONFIRM: pricing model — per lb / per box / per minute]`.
2. Weigh or estimate volume; quote before accepting.
3. Ask them to **remove** large binder clips, hardback binders, or anything our
   shredder can't handle `[CONFIRM: what's accepted — staples/paper clips
   usually OK]`.

## 2. Two service models

**On‑site shredder (we shred now):**
- Shred while the customer waits if they want to witness it.
- Don't read the documents; feed and destroy.
- Empty shred bin into the appropriate recycling/disposal per policy.

**Secure bin / third‑party pickup:**
- Place documents directly into the **locked** shred bin.
- Never store unsecured customer documents awaiting pickup out in the open.
- A bonded shredding vendor picks up and destroys; offer a **Certificate of
  Destruction** if the customer needs one `[CONFIRM: available?]`.

## 3. Confidentiality rules

- Documents to be shredded are confidential. Don't read, copy, photograph, or
  remove anything from them.
- If a customer accidentally includes something they clearly didn't mean to
  shred (e.g., a passport, cash), stop and return it.
- Misprints, voided labels, and our own paperwork containing customer personal
  info also go to shred — never the open trash.

## 4. Receipt

- Provide a receipt for the shredding service, and a Certificate of Destruction
  when offered/requested.

> The product is security. Keep documents locked, don't read them, destroy them
> as promised, and give proof when asked.
