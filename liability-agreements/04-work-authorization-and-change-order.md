### Note on hidden conditions
If, during the Work, the Company encounters a concealed condition (e.g., rot,
corroded/aged plumbing, outdated or unsafe wiring, mold, asbestos, structural
issue) that affects the scope, cost, safety, or timeline, the Company will pause
and notify Customer. Continued Work, added cost, or a referral to a licensed
specialist requires a signed Change Order above. The Company is not responsible
for the existence of such conditions (see MSA §5 and Doc 03).
