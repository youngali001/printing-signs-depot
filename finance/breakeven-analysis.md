# Cross Creek Pak N Ship — Breakeven & Profit Targets

**Prepared:** 2026-06-28 · Review whenever rent, payroll, or the loan changes.

> **What "income" means here.** All targets are measured against the money the
> business actually **keeps** — USPS CPU commission + service fees (notary,
> passport, fax, copies) + supply sales. That's the same figure the close-out
> app reports as **"Est. total income."** Materials are counted as an expense
> below, so don't double-count them.

## Monthly fixed costs

| Expense | Monthly |
|---|---|
| Rent | $2,009 |
| Electric (lights) | $800 |
| Phone | $300 |
| Payroll (employee) | $2,700 |
| Materials | $500 |
| Credit-card processing fee | $800 |
| **Operating subtotal** | **$7,109** |
| Loan payment (range) | $500 – $1,000 |
| **Total monthly** | **$7,609 – $8,109** |

Loan principal outstanding: **$35,000** (paid down over time at $500–$1,000/mo).

## Breakeven — cover everything, $0 profit

| | Low (loan $500/mo) | High (loan $1,000/mo) |
|---|---|---|
| Per month | $7,609 | $8,109 |
| **Per year** | **$91,308** | **$97,308** |
| Per open day (~25 days/mo) | ~$300 | ~$320 |

**Need to average ~$300–$320/day in income just to keep the doors open.**

## 100% profit — double your money (keep profit equal to total costs)

| | Low | High |
|---|---|---|
| Per month | $15,218 | $16,218 |
| **Per year** | **$182,616** | **$194,616** |
| Per open day | ~$600 | ~$640 |

**~$600–$640/day** means that after paying every bill and the loan, you pocket
about as much as you spent — a true 100% return for the year.

## How to read it day to day

| Daily income | Result |
|---|---|
| Under ~$300 | Losing money |
| ~$300–$320 | Breaking even |
| Each $1/day above ~$320 | Profit (≈ $300/day extra ≈ ~$7.5K/yr to your pocket) |
| ~$600+ | Doubled your money for the year |

## Caveats

1. **Taxes are not included.** Set aside roughly **25–30%** of net profit for
   taxes, so the real "take-home" target is a bit above the 100%-profit line.
2. Figures assume a **full 12 months**. For only the **remainder of 2026**
   (~6 months) the breakeven is about half: roughly **$45K–$49K** more income.
3. Open-day math assumes ~25 open days/month (Mon–Fri + Sat).

## Assumptions to revisit

- Payroll is a single employee at $2,700/mo.
- CC processing fee (~$800/mo) scales with card volume — it will rise as sales
  rise; revisit if card mix changes.
- Loan payment treated as cash out, not split into principal/interest.
