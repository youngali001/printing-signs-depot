## Core principles (apply to everything)

1. **Greet within 10 seconds.** Make eye contact, smile, say "Welcome to Cross
   Creek Pak N Ship." Even if you're with another customer, acknowledge the
   next person.
2. **Quote before you charge.** Always tell the customer the price *before* you
   run the transaction. No surprises at the register.
3. **Every package gets a receipt and tracking.** The customer leaves with
   proof of what they shipped and how to track it.
4. **Never guarantee a delivery date** unless the carrier service guarantees it
   in writing (e.g., USPS Priority Mail Express, UPS Next Day Air). Say
   "estimated."
5. **Handle ID and documents like they're valuable — because they are.** Never
   leave a customer's passport, ID, or originals unattended on the counter.
6. **When unsure, ask.** Call the manager before improvising on a refund,
   claim, notary, or anything involving cash over `[CONFIRM: amount, e.g. $100]`.
7. **The store is always presentable.** If you have time to lean, you have time
   to clean, restock, and face the shelves.
