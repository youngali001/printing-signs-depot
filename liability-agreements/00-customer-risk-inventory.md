# Customer Risk Inventory — Everything a Customer Might Make a Problem Over

> Not legal advice. See [`README.md`](README.md) for the full disclaimer.

This is the master checklist of the things customers actually complain, dispute,
withhold payment over, leave bad reviews over, or sue over in the handyman /
home-services trade. Each item lists **the risk**, **how it shows up**, and
**which agreement section shifts or limits it**. Use it to make sure nothing is
left uncovered.

Legend for "Covered by":
**MSA** = Master Service Agreement (01) ·
**WR** = Waiver & Release (02) ·
**PEC** = Pre-Existing Conditions (03) ·
**WA/CO** = Work Authorization / Change Order (04) ·
**WAR** = Limited Warranty (05) ·
**CSM** = Customer-Supplied Materials (06) ·
**SIGN** = Completion Sign-Off (07)

---

## 1. Property damage (the #1 source of claims)

| # | What the customer complains about | Covered by |
|---|-----------------------------------|-----------|
| 1.1 | Scratches, dents, scuffs to floors, walls, trim, countertops while working/moving tools | PEC, MSA (limitation), SIGN |
| 1.2 | Damage to surrounding/adjacent areas not part of the job (overspray, drips, dust on belongings) | MSA, WR |
| 1.3 | Breaking a hidden pipe, wire, duct, or cable concealed inside a wall/floor/ceiling | MSA (concealed conditions), WA/CO |
| 1.4 | Cracking drywall/plaster/tile that was already brittle or under tension | PEC, MSA |
| 1.5 | Damage to driveways, landscaping, sprinkler lines, sod from equipment/vehicles | PEC, MSA |
| 1.6 | Damage to customer's appliances, electronics, HVAC tied into the work | MSA, CSM |
| 1.7 | A repair that later **fails** and causes secondary damage (leak → water/mold; wiring → fire) | MSA (consequential-damage exclusion), WAR |
| 1.8 | Damage caused by moving the customer's furniture/items to access the work area | MSA, WR, PEC |
| 1.9 | Paint/finish/grout/caulk color or texture not matching existing | WA/CO, WAR (cosmetic exclusion) |

## 12. Process / consent gaps (how you LOSE the protections above)

These aren't customer complaints — they're the mistakes that make every clause
above unenforceable. Treat them as a pre-job checklist:

- ❌ Agreement signed *after* work started, or not signed at all → get it signed **first**.
- ❌ Blanks not filled in (no cap amount, no claim window, no signatures/initials).
- ❌ Key limitations hidden in fine print → use clear headings + **initials**.
- ❌ Verbal change to scope with no written change order → use WA/CO every time.
- ❌ No record of the property's pre-work condition → photos + PEC sheet.
- ❌ Using the documents inconsistently (only on "risky" jobs) → use every time.
- ❌ Promising more verbally than the contract says → the writing controls; don't oversell.
- ❌ No / lapsed insurance or license → contracts don't replace coverage.

---

### Quick-start: minimum set for a typical job
1. **Pre-Existing Conditions** sheet + photos (03)
2. **Master Service Agreement** signed before work (01)
3. **Work Authorization** for the specific scope (04)
4. **Completion Sign-Off** + **Limited Warranty** at the end (07, 05)

Add the **Waiver & Release** (02) and **Customer-Supplied Materials** (06) when
the job is higher-risk or the customer provides parts.
