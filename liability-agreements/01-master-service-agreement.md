maintenance* services; they are not a guarantee of any particular result,
appearance, code compliance of pre-existing conditions, or future performance of
aging systems.

## 3. Customer Responsibilities & Warranties
You represent, warrant, and agree that:
1. You own the property or are legally authorized to approve the Work, and

2. You have disclosed all known hazards and concealed conditions (wiring,
   plumbing, gas lines, asbestos, lead, mold, structural issues, pets, etc.).
3. You will provide safe, clear, and timely access to the work area, plus
   working utilities as needed.
4. You will **remove or secure all valuables, fragile items, irreplaceable or
   sentimental property, cash, jewelry, electronics, and important data** from

5. You will keep children, pets, and other occupants away from active work areas.

The Company is **not responsible** for:
1. **Pre-existing conditions** — any damage, defect, wear, code violation, or

4. **Cosmetic variation** — exact matching of paint, stain, grout, caulk, tile,
   or finishes to existing/aged materials is not guaranteed.
5. **Aging systems** — repairs to old or deteriorated systems may not restore
   them to like-new condition or prevent future failure elsewhere.

7. **Permits/code on existing work** — bringing pre-existing, non-conforming
