## 4. Concealed-condition acknowledgment
Customer understands that conditions hidden inside walls, floors, ceilings, or
behind fixtures (wiring, plumbing, gas, rot, mold, asbestos, lead, pest/structural
damage) cannot be fully assessed in advance, and that discovering them may require
a Change Order (Doc 04), additional cost, or stopping Work. The Company is not
responsible for the existence of such conditions or for damage that results from
their pre-existing failure.
