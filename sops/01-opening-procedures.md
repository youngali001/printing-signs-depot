# SOP 01 — Opening Procedures

**Goal:** Store is unlocked, safe, stocked, and ready to serve the first
customer at opening time. Arrive at least **`[CONFIRM: 20–30]` minutes** before
opening.

## 1. Enter & disarm

1. Unlock the front door and re‑lock it behind you (do not open to customers
   yet).
2. Disarm the alarm within the grace period. Code: `[CONFIRM]`.
3. Turn on the lights, open signs, and any "OPEN" neon/LED.
4. Do a quick walk‑through: check for overnight issues (leaks, break‑in signs,
   anything out of place). If anything looks wrong, **do not touch it** — call
   the manager.

## 2. Power up systems

1. Turn on and log into the POS / register. Confirm it connects.
2. Power on shipping scales and confirm they read **0.00** with nothing on them
   (zero/tare them if not).
3. Power on the label printer(s), receipt printer, copier/printer, and computer.
4. Print a test label and a test copy to confirm both work and have ink/toner.
5. Log into carrier systems: USPS CPU terminal, UPS, FedEx, DHL.
6. Confirm internet is up.

## 3. Cash drawer

1. Retrieve the till from the safe.
2. Count the starting bank. It should equal **`[CONFIRM: e.g. $150]`**.
3. Record the count on the [Cash Handling log](10-cash-handling-pos.md).
4. If the count is off by more than **`[CONFIRM: $2]`**, recount; if still off,
   note it and notify the manager before opening.

## 4. Stock & stage

1. Restock supplies at each station: boxes, envelopes, tape, bubble wrap,
   packing peanuts, labels, pens, tape guns.
2. Refill the printer/copier paper trays.
3. Stage outbound shipments left from the previous day so they're ready for
   carrier pickup.
4. Empty/relocate any overnight drop‑box returns to the staging area.

## 5. Storefront

1. Wipe down the counter, glass door, and POS area.
2. Empty trash if needed; check restroom (if customer‑facing).
3. Bring in / put out any sidewalk signage `[CONFIRM]`.
4. Face shelves and straighten retail displays (boxes, mailing supplies,
   greeting cards, etc.).

## 6. Open

1. At opening time exactly, flip the sign to **OPEN** and unlock the door.
2. Check voicemail and email; note any callbacks or held‑package requests.

> **Opening checklist** (initial each morning): alarm off · POS up · scales
> zeroed · label + copier tested · till counted · stations stocked · storefront
> clean · sign flipped.
