# SOP 08 — Printing, Copies, Fax & Scanning

Covers self‑/full‑service copies and prints, faxing, and scanning. These are
fast, frequent jobs — accuracy and a clear price are what matter.

> **Price list:** Fax **$1.50 per page**. Copies **$1 for 1–3 pages**, **$3 for
> 4–10 pages** `[CONFIRM: price for 11+ pages]`. Record fax pages and copy jobs
> on the end-of-day close-out so they're tracked in daily income.

## 1. Copies & printing

1. Confirm the job: **size** (Letter/Legal/Tabloid), **color or B&W**,
   **single‑ or double‑sided**, **quantity**, and any finishing (staple, fold,
   laminate, cut).
2. For files: accept from USB, email, cloud link, or our station `[CONFIRM:
   accepted methods]`. Scan USB drives are a malware risk — use the designated
   print station, not the POS computer.
3. Quote per the price list `[CONFIRM: per‑page B&W / color / large format]`.
4. Run a **single test print first** for large or color jobs and have the
   customer approve before printing the full run.
5. Print, finish, collect payment, hand over the job.

### Quality & reprints
- If a print is our error (smudge, wrong settings, jam), reprint at no charge
  and recycle/shred the bad copy.
- If the customer's file is the problem (low resolution, wrong size), show them
  before printing the full run so they decide.

### Copyright
- Decline to copy items that are obviously **copyrighted** (currency, certain
  certified documents, books/materials marked "do not copy") where prohibited.
  When unsure, ask the manager.

## 2. Fax

1. Confirm **destination number** (with country/area code) and number of pages.
2. Quote: `[CONFIRM: first page / additional page; domestic vs. international]`.
3. Send the fax; **wait for the confirmation page**.
4. Give the customer the confirmation as proof; charge once confirmed sent.
5. If it fails, retry once; if still failing, don't charge for the failed
   attempt and tell the customer.
6. Hand back all original documents.

## 3. Scanning / digital

1. Confirm format (PDF/JPEG), resolution, and delivery method (email, USB).
2. Scan; verify pages are legible, in order, and complete.
3. Send/save to the customer's destination, then **delete the file from our
   equipment** — don't retain customer documents.
4. Charge per the price list `[CONFIRM]`.

## 4. Handling customer documents & data

- Treat every document as confidential. Don't read beyond what's needed to do
  the job; don't discuss contents.
- Return **all originals** before the customer leaves — count pages back to
  them.
- Shred any misprints/test pages containing personal info
  ([SOP 09](09-shredding.md)).
- Don't store customer files on store equipment longer than needed to complete
  the job.

> Confirm the spec, quote the price, test before a big run, return every
> original, and don't keep customers' files.
